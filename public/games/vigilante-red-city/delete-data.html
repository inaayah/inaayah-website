Delete Vigilante Red City data

Vigilante: Red City is published by Inaayah Studio, Bangladesh (BD). Privacy requests: privacy@inaayah.dev.

Document version: 0.3.1. Effective date: 2026-11-01.

Delete your online profile in the game

  1. Leave the current Internet Room.
  2. Open Settings, then Privacy & Data.
  3. Choose Delete Online Profile and confirm.
  4. Wait for the server-confirmed result. If the request fails, retry when the service is reachable or contact us below.

This deletes the installation's anonymous online profile and its associated account data. It does not delete your offline campaign progress, collectibles or settings. Disabling Internet Rooms only stops that feature; it does not delete the profile.

If your installed version does not include Privacy & Data, use the external request option below. Profile deletion is not postponed to keep support correspondence for a fixed period.

Request deletion without reinstalling

Email privacy@inaayah.dev with the subject “Vigilante Red City data deletion.” This route is available even after uninstalling and does not require a new account.

  • State whether you want the online profile, previous support correspondence, or both deleted.
  • Include your Online Profile ID if you have it. While the game is installed, Privacy & Data offers Copy Online Profile ID.
  • If you do not have the ID, explain that. We will discuss whether we can identify the record safely without requiring a reinstall.

The Online Profile ID is a lookup reference, not a sign-in secret or sufficient proof of ownership. We may need limited additional information to verify a request. Do not send an installation sign-in identifier, session token, password, keystore, payment-card number or identity document in an ordinary email. We will explain if we cannot safely identify or verify the requested record.

We process requests promptly and follow applicable legal deadlines. We will confirm the outcome or explain a lawful limitation.

What remains and for how long

Origin diagnostic logs can retain account or connection references for up to the seven-day cleanup target. Cloudflare security records follow separate provider retention; its Security Events documentation currently describes up to 31 days of history, not a universal limit for every provider record. Relevant support and store records can have separate legal or operational retention requirements. See the privacy policy.

Ending a room removes active room state when closure is detected. The service does not store match replays or cloud campaign backups. Account deletion does not erase other players' local save files.

Remove local saves

On Android, clearing the game's application storage removes this installation's local saves, collectibles, settings and sign-in information. This can be irreversible. Delete the online profile first if you also want its server record removed; clearing application storage alone does not send a deletion request. Android settings names can vary by device.

For other platforms, contact support@inaayah.dev if you need help finding local save data. Read the terms of use for service and support expectations.
