Vigilante Red City privacy policy

Publisher: Inaayah Studio, Bangladesh (BD). Privacy contact: privacy@inaayah.dev. Game support: support@inaayah.dev.

Document version: 0.3.1. Effective date: 2026-11-01.

This policy covers Vigilante: Red City, including its optional Internet Rooms service. It explains what stays on your device, what is sent when you play online, and how to request deletion.

Playing on your device

Campaign progress, collectibles, settings and your Internet Rooms privacy choice are stored on your device. Internet Rooms does not upload or synchronize your campaign saves or collectibles. Online co-op checkpoints are saved on the host's device. To continue together, the host loads that checkpoint and the other player joins a new room.

Removing application data, uninstalling, changing devices or losing the device can remove your local progress. Internet Rooms does not provide cloud-save recovery. Solo and local co-op do not require an Internet Rooms profile.

Optional Internet Rooms

Internet Rooms connects only after you choose to enable it. The game creates a random installation sign-in identifier and sends it to our service to create an anonymous profile. The service assigns a separate profile ID and uses session credentials to authenticate connections.

The service processes connection IP addresses, room codes, selected stages, character and readiness selections, connection status, and co-op gameplay packets to find rooms and run the game. A room's code and selected stage can be visible to other players browsing available rooms. Room participants receive the information needed to play together.

Anonymous does not mean that no data is collected. The installation identifier can recognize the same installation over time. It is a sign-in credential, not a name, email address, advertising identifier or hardware serial number. The separate online profile ID is a support reference, not proof of ownership.

The current game has no player chat, custom room names, player media uploads, advertising SDK, advertising tracking or in-game AI generation. The game does not request your contacts, microphone, camera or device location for gameplay.

Local network play

Wi-Fi/LAN discovery and co-op exchange local network addresses, room and gameplay information directly between devices. These connections use local UDP/ENet traffic. They do not use our Internet Rooms profile service and are not described as encrypted Internet Rooms connections. Play on a network you trust.

Why we process online data

We use online data to authenticate your installation, list and join rooms, coordinate co-op play, maintain the service, investigate faults and protect against abuse. We do not sell game-profile data or use it for targeted advertising.

Enabling Internet Rooms is a product choice. Any additional permission or parental authorization required by applicable law must be handled separately; this choice is not a substitute for it.

Providers and purchases

Our game servers run on hosting supplied by Hetzner. Cloudflare provides Internet traffic protection and proxying. These providers process connection and service data to deliver and secure the service. Service processing can take place outside your country, under the providers' applicable safeguards and terms.

Google Play processes Play Store purchases and downloads under its own policies. The current game does not collect payment-card details or use an in-app billing SDK. Information available to the publisher through store purchase or support systems is separate from the game-profile service and may have legal retention requirements.

If you email us, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards the message to our monitored Gmail mailbox. Cloudflare and Google process your email address, message and anything you choose to attach to provide those services. Avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

On the game website, the optional trailer connects to YouTube only when you press Play or follow the YouTube link. YouTube then processes connection and playback information under Google's privacy policy. This website video is separate from the game's Internet Rooms choice and is not needed to play the game.

The website also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and page performance. Cloudflare processes website request and browser-performance information for this measurement and for security. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as cookie-free and not intended to track individuals across websites; see Cloudflare Web Analytics. This website measurement is separate from the Android game and does not upload your campaign saves or add an in-game analytics SDK. The policy and deletion instructions remain readable without JavaScript.

Retention

  • Local saves and settings stay on your device until removed by you, the application or the operating system. We cannot remotely recover or erase those files through Internet Rooms.
  • An anonymous online profile remains until deleted or otherwise removed from the service. Closing a room, disabling Internet Rooms or uninstalling the game does not itself delete the server profile. Profiles are not guaranteed to remain available indefinitely.
  • Active room state and gameplay relay data are processed for the room session. A room closes when its host leaves or the service detects that it has ended. The service is not a stored match-replay or campaign-save service.
  • Origin diagnostic logs have a maximum retention target of seven days, with automated age and size cleanup configured to expire them earlier. Warnings and errors can contain profile, session or connection identifiers. These logs are separate from online profiles.
  • Cloudflare security records have separate provider retention. Cloudflare currently documents Security Events history of up to 31 days. This is not a promise that every category of Cloudflare data has a 31-day maximum. See Cloudflare Security Events and Cloudflare privacy information.
  • Support correspondence is retained to handle the request, follow-up and applicable legal obligations. The origin-log cleanup period does not apply to email or store transaction records. You can request deletion of correspondence where applicable.

Deleting an online profile is not delayed by a support-correspondence retention period. We do not keep a copy of the deleted profile in a support message as a substitute for deleting it. Any necessary completion record is limited to what is needed to handle the request or meet a legal obligation.

The game-profile service currently has no database backup system. Credential backups and configuration rollback copies do not back up player records or campaign progress. Online profiles and rooms may be unavailable or lost; keep this in mind when relying on online features.

Delete your data

In the game, open Settings, then Privacy & Data, leave any online room, and choose Delete Online Profile. The game reports success only after the server confirms deletion or confirms that the profile no longer exists. If deletion fails, the local sign-in information is kept so you can retry. This removes the anonymous online profile and associated account data; it leaves local campaign saves, collectibles and settings intact.

If your installed version does not include Privacy & Data, use the external request option below.

You can also request deletion by emailing privacy@inaayah.dev. Include the game name and, if available, your Online Profile ID. You do not need to reinstall the game or create a new account to contact us. The data deletion page explains what to send and what deletion covers.

We may ask for limited additional information needed to find the profile and verify that you are entitled to make the request. A public profile ID alone does not authorize deletion. Do not send your installation sign-in identifier, session token, password, keystore or identity document in an ordinary support email. If you no longer have the profile reference, describe the situation and we will explain whether the record can be identified safely.

Account deletion does not automatically remove diagnostic or provider security records before their separate retention periods expire. Where information must be retained for a lawful reason, we will explain the applicable limitation. We handle requests promptly and within deadlines required by applicable law.

Your choices and rights

You can leave online rooms, disable Internet Rooms, delete the online profile, and remove local application data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to the relevant authority. Contact privacy@inaayah.dev to make a request.

Children and parents

The current intended audience is ages 13 and older, subject to the store's regional content rating. This is separate from the age at which a person can independently consent to personal-data processing under local law. Where parental authorization is required, a child's own Internet Rooms choice does not replace it. Parents or guardians can contact privacy@inaayah.dev about a child's data.

Security and changes

Internet Rooms uses HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Access to service administration and credentials is restricted. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that a device, network or online service cannot fail or be compromised.

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change, with a new version and effective date. Material changes requiring a new notice or choice will be reflected in the game before the affected collection starts. Terms of use describe the game licence and online-service expectations.
